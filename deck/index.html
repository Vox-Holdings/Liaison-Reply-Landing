HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from stuck to sent.

Step 1: Paste the message

Drop in the text you don't know how to answer.

Step 2: Pick the relationship context

What Liaison Reply does.

  • Generates a set of reply options for each message you paste. Pick the direction that fits: de-escalate, connect, or hold your ground.

FAQ

Questions before you start.

Is Liaison Reply a chatbot?

No. It's a drafting tool: you paste a message, get reply options, edit if needed, and copy the one you want back into your real conversation.

How do I cancel Pro?

Through your Stripe billing page, anytime. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Where do I use it?
