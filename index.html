How it works

Step 1

Paste the message, or drop in a screenshot

Drop in the text you don’t know how to answer, or upload a screenshot of the conversation and Liaison Reply reads it in for you.

Step 2

Pick the relationship context

The app asks you to select a relationship context before it generates anything, so the drafts fit the actual situation. You also pick the direction: calm it down, set a boundary, buy time, close the loop, or your own steer in your own words.

Step 3

Choose, edit, send

Review the reply options, tweak the wording, and copy the one you want back into your own conversation. Liaison Reply doesn’t send anything for you. You stay in control.

What it does, and what it doesn’t

What it does

  • Reads the whole conversation, pasted in or from a screenshot, and drafts three reply options for the message you need to answer.
  • Coaches you while you edit, pointing out wording that could land wrong before you send.
  • Polishes a draft you wrote yourself, when you have the words but not the wording.
  • Tunes the drafts to the relationship context and the writing persona you select.
  • Your threads are private to you. Only you can read, edit, or delete them.
  • Your first 15 generations are free, and the app shows your remaining credits live as you go.

What it doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t send messages for you. You copy the reply you pick and send it yourself.
  • It doesn’t pull messages from your accounts. Nothing is read until you paste it or share a screenshot.
  • It never asks for access to your inbox, your contacts, or your messaging accounts.
  • It’s not a chatbot you converse with. It’s a drafting tool for messages you already received.

Memory

The conversation does not reset.

Every thread keeps its history, so your drafts know what was said last week, not just the last message. On Pro, threads are never summarized down. The full conversation stays in play.

Some situations live in more than one conversation: the same problem with your partner, your manager, and your client. Link the threads, and Pro drafts draw on all of them, so the reply fits the whole situation instead of one slice of it.
