What it does, and what it doesn’t

What it does

  • Generates a set of reply options for each message you paste. Pick the direction that fits: de-escalate, connect, or hold your ground.
  • Calibrates drafts to the relationship context you select.
  • Shows your remaining free credits live in the app as you go.
  • Your threads are private to you. Only you can read, edit, or delete them.
  • Billing and subscription changes are handled by you, directly through Stripe.

What it doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t send messages for you.
  • It’s not a chatbot you converse with. It’s a drafting tool for messages you already received.
