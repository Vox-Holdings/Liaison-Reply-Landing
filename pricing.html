Questions

Before you upgrade

Why not just use ChatGPT?

You can, but you will be writing the prompt every time. Liaison Reply is a drafting tool built for one job: answering a hard message. You pick the relationship context and a direction (De-escalate, Set a boundary, Buy time, Get clarity, Close the loop, Keep momentum) and get three reply options. Strategic coaching flags risky wording while you edit, and each thread keeps its history, so the next draft knows what was said last week. On Pro, threads are never summarized down and you can link related conversations. It is not a chatbot you talk to. It is the read on the room, then the words.

What happens to the messages I share?

Your threads are private to you. Only you can read, edit, or delete them. Liaison Reply never asks for access to your inbox, contacts, or messaging accounts, and it reads nothing until you share a screenshot or type it in. It never sends anything for you. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What happens when my 15 free generations run out?

Your threads stay where they are. To keep generating, earn bonus credits or upgrade to Pro for unlimited generations. You will find your referral code in Settings, under Invite & Earn Credits. When a friend joins with your code and verifies their email, you get 5 extra credits and they get 2 extra credits. Bonus credits add on top of your 15. The app shows your remaining credits live, so it is never a surprise.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you were charged in the last 14 days, email support@liaisonreply.com and we will refund that charge in full, no questions asked. After 14 days, amounts already paid are non-refundable unless applicable law requires otherwise. See the Terms.

How do I cancel?

From your Stripe billing page, anytime. No need to call anyone. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

Monthly or annual?

Annual is $79.99/year, about $6.67 a month, which saves about 17% versus paying $7.99/month. Both billing options include everything in Pro and the same 14-day refund.

Where do I use it?

In your browser at app.liaisonreply.com. Nothing to download.
