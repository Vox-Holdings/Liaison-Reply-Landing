How to reject someone nicely over text kind, clear, and only once.

Short answer: thank them, say clearly that you're not feeling a romantic connection, and wish them well. Keep it to two or three sentences, skip fake reasons and maybes, and don't reopen it if they ask why. Clear is kinder than slowly fading out.

01 Copy and send

Texts to turn someone down nicely

A kind rejection text does three things in a few lines: thanks them, says no clearly, and ends warmly. Pick the one that matches how far things went, change the [brackets], and send it once.

Kind and clear, after a few dates

The default. Honest without a list of reasons.

Hey [Name], I've had a nice time getting to know you, and I want to be honest: I'm not feeling a romantic connection. You're genuinely great, and I wish you the best.

Before a first date, on a dating app

Short is fine when you have only been chatting.

Hey [Name], thanks for the chats. I've realized I'm not feeling it the way I'd want to before meeting up, so I'm going to bow out. Good luck out there!

Turning down someone you know

A friend, a classmate or a coworker. Protect the friendship.

Hey [Name], I really appreciate you telling me how you feel, and that took guts. I don't feel the same way, but I value you as a [friend] and I'd like that to stay the same.

After they ask you out again

When a soft no did not land.

Hi [Name], I should have been clearer last time, sorry. I'm not interested in dating, and I don't want to leave you guessing. I wish you all the best.

Letting someone down easy after a situationship

Things were casual but regular.

Hey [Name], I've enjoyed our time together, and I've realized I'm not looking to keep this going. I'd rather tell you than drift away. Thank you for the good weeks.

02 After a bad first date

What to text after a first date when you are not interested

You don't owe a long explanation after one date, but a short text beats silence, especially if they've already messaged to say they had fun. Send it within a day or two, keep it kind, and don't critique the date itself.

If the date felt unsafe or disrespectful, you don't owe a message at all. Unmatching or blocking is a reasonable choice.

They texted first that they had fun

Answer warmly, then be clear.

Thanks for tonight, [Name], and thanks for picking the spot. I had a nice time, but I didn't feel a romantic spark. I'd rather be upfront than leave you guessing. Take care!

Not interested after the first date, no text yet

Send it the next day.

Hi [Name], thanks again for [coffee] yesterday. I enjoyed meeting you, but I don't think we're a match. Wishing you the best.

The date was awkward for both of you

A light touch, no post mortem.

Hey [Name], thanks for meeting up. I don't think we clicked, and I'm guessing you might feel the same. Good luck with the search!

Not sure whether to text after a bad first date at all? If neither of you has messaged and it was clearly mutual, silence is usually fine. If they reached out, reply.

03 If they push back

What to say when someone asks why you are rejecting them

Jordan's reply is hurt, a little stung and asking for a reason. It is a normal reaction, and it is also the moment people tend to backpedal or over-explain. Neither helps him.

He wants a reason. A reason would only give him something to argue with.

When someone asks what they did wrong, the kind answer is that they did nothing wrong. Keep the no, close the loop, and let the conversation end gently.
  • “Can I at least ask what I did wrong?”Answer the fear, not the question. He didn't do anything wrong.
  • “You literally said you wanted to try the ramen place.”He's pointing to mixed signals. You can own that without reopening the door.
  • “I'd honestly rather you just tell me.”An invitation to list flaws. Don't. Chemistry is a complete reason.

Why this works: short, kind and final, which is often the most respectful version.

Not sure which to send? After two dates, Safe or Warm fits most people. If he keeps asking for reasons after this, Direct is the one to repeat.

When they reply again, and what to say

Most people take one clear text well. If they don't, here are calm closing lines.

  • “Can we just be friends then?”

    That's kind of you to offer. I think a clean break is better for both of us right now, but I really do wish you well.

  • “Give me one more date to change your mind.”

    I appreciate that, but my answer won't change, and I don't want to waste your evening.

  • “Is there someone else?”

    No, it's just not the connection I'm looking for. Take care, Jordan.

  • “You're making a mistake.”

    Maybe, but I have to go with how I feel. I hope you find someone great.

  • “Wow, okay. Good to know.”

What not to say when you turn someone down

The most common mistake is softening a no until it sounds like a maybe. This edit of the Warm reply shows three lines strategic coaching flags, and why each one keeps him hoping.

Live coachingCaution

Your edit of the Warm reply

I'm so sorry, I feel terrible. You didn't do anything wrong, I'm just really busy right now. Maybe if things calm down.

Phrase notes

  • I'm so sorry, I feel terribleHeavy guilt makes him comfort you, which flips the roles.Try: I've really enjoyed getting to know you
  • I'm just really busy right nowA fake reason has a fix, so he will wait for you to be less busy.Try: I'm not feeling a romantic connection
  • Maybe if things calm downA maybe is an invitation to ask again in a month.Try: I don't see this going further

Also leave out

  • A critique of the date. Their jokes, their outfit, the restaurant. None of it helps.
  • Comparisons to someone else. Even a gentle one lands as a ranking.
  • Ghosting after they asked directly. If they asked where things stand, a short answer is kinder than silence.
  • A wall of text. Three sentences is plenty. Long rejections feel like a debate.

06 FAQ

Rejecting someone over text, answered

How do you reject someone nicely over text?

Thank them, say plainly that you're not feeling a romantic connection, and wish them well. Two or three sentences is enough. Avoid fake excuses and maybes.

Is it okay to reject someone over text?

Yes, for early dating and app matches it is normal and often preferred. After a longer relationship, a call or a conversation in person is kinder.

How do you tell someone you're not interested after the first date?

Send a short text within a day or two: thanks for the date, you didn't feel a spark, you wish them well. You don't need to explain or critique anything.

What do you say if they ask why you're not interested?

Tell them they didn't do anything wrong and that it's about chemistry. Specific flaws only invite argument and rarely help.

How do you let someone down easy without leading them on?

Be warm about them as a person and clear about the answer. Skip “maybe later”, “I'm just busy” and offers to hang out that you don't mean.

Should you text after a bad first date?

If they messaged you, reply with a kind, clear no. If neither of you reached out and it was obviously mutual, silence is usually fine.

How do you reject someone you know as a friend?

Thank them for telling you, say you don't feel the same, and say clearly that you value the friendship. Then act normal, so the friendship can recover.

What if they keep texting after you said no?

Repeat your answer once, briefly. If it continues, you can stop replying, mute or block. You don't owe ongoing explanations.
