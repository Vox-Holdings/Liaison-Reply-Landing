01 Copy and send

How to reply to your ex depending on what you want

There is no single right way to respond to an ex. The right reply depends on whether you want distance, friendship, or to explore getting back together. Pick the template that matches your answer, not theirs.

You want distance, politely

Friendly, short, and nothing to reply to.

Hi [Name], thanks for the message. I'm doing well, and I hope you are too. Take care.

You want no contact

Clear, once, then you can stop replying.

Hi [Name], I hope you're doing okay. I've realized I need no contact to move on, so I won't be replying going forward. I wish you well.

You are open to staying friends

Warm, with a boundary on what friends means.

Hey [Name], good to hear from you. I'd be open to being friendly, but I want to be clear it would just be as friends. How have you been?

You want to know why they are reaching out

Curious, without committing to a drink.

Hi [Name], that's a surprise. I'm doing well. What's on your mind?

You need time to think

You can answer later, or not at all.

Hey [Name], I got your message. I need a little time to think about whether catching up is a good idea for me. I'll let you know.

02 Holiday texts from an ex

When your ex texts you happy new year or merry christmas

Holiday texts are low effort to send and often mean less than they feel like. Some exes send one to everyone in their contacts at midnight. Others use it as a soft test of whether the door is open. A short reply in kind is polite. Not replying is also fine.

If the holiday message is a pattern every year and it unsettles you, the no contact template above works at any time of year.

Ex texted happy new year

Polite and complete.

Thanks, [Name]. Happy New Year to you too. I hope it's a good one.

Ex texted merry christmas

Warm without inviting a chat.

Merry Christmas, [Name]. Hope you have a relaxing break.

Ex texted on new year’s eve and wants to talk

Kind, and a clear pause.

Happy New Year, [Name]. I'm glad you're doing well, but I'm not looking to reconnect. Wishing you a good year ahead.

A midnight message right after a breakup lands harder. It is okay to read it the next morning and decide then.

03 Read the text first

What it might mean when your ex texts you out of the blue

Sam's text has nostalgia, a hint about feelings and a specific invite, all wrapped in “random”. You can't know for sure what an ex means. You can decide what you want before you answer.

He's testing the water. You get to choose whether to step in.

An ex reaching out is information, not an obligation. Buying time gives you room to answer from what you want now, not what you felt then.
  • “Random, but I thought of you.”“Random” keeps it low stakes for him. It doesn't have to be low stakes for you.
  • “I've been thinking a lot lately.”Vague on purpose. If it matters, he can say it clearly.
  • “Grab a drink sometime and catch up?”A real ask. You don't have to answer it tonight.

Direction Buy time

Safe reply

Hey Sam, that's nice of you to say. I'm doing well, thanks. I'm not sure about getting a drink yet. Let me think about it.

Why this works: polite, honest about being unsure, and it doesn't commit you to anything.

Warm reply

Hi Sam, that place has good memories. I'm glad you're doing okay. Honestly, I need a bit of time before I decide whether catching up is a good idea for me. I'll get back to you.

Why this works: it acknowledges the shared history kindly, then gives you room without leaving him guessing about whether you saw it.

Direct reply

Hey Sam, thanks for reaching out. Before I say yes to a drink, what's been on your mind?

Why this works: it asks him to say plainly what he means, so your decision is based on facts.

Not sure which to send? If you need space to think, Safe or Warm. If you suspect he wants to get back together and you want to know before deciding, Direct.

When your ex replies, what to say next

Your first reply sets the pace. Here is how to hold it.

  • “I miss you.”

    I appreciate you telling me. I need to think about what I want before I say anything back.

  • “Why are you being so cold?”

    I'm not trying to be cold. I'm just being careful, and I'd rather take my time.

  • “Can I call you instead?”

    I'd prefer to keep it to text for now.

  • “I've changed, I promise.”

    I'm glad you're doing well. That doesn't change that I need time to decide what's right for me.

  • “Just one drink, no pressure.”

    I hear you. My answer is not right now. I'll reach out if that changes.

What not to say when your ex texts you

A text from an ex can pull out old feelings fast. This edit of the Warm reply contains three lines strategic coaching flags, and what each one gives away.

Your edit of the Warm reply

Phrase notes

  • OMG hi!!Matching his energy instantly skips the part where you decide what you want.Try: Hey
  • I literally think about you all the timeA big reveal in the first reply hands him the next move.Try: It's been a while
  • I'm free any night this weekIt says yes to the drink before you know why he wants one.Try: What's on your mind?

Also leave out

  • Replying while emotional or after a drink. Read it, sleep on it, answer tomorrow if at all.
  • Rehashing the breakup over text. If it needs saying, it needs a real conversation.
  • Asking about their new partner. It signals interest you may not mean.
  • Replying because you feel guilty. You can be kind and still not reply.

06 FAQ

When your ex texts you, answered

What should you say when your ex texts you?

Decide what you want first. For distance, a short polite reply or none. For friendship, say so clearly. If you're curious, ask what's on their mind before agreeing to meet.

Should you reply when your ex texts you?

You don't have to. Not replying is a valid choice, especially if you need no contact to move on. If you do reply, keep it brief until you know what you want.

Why would an ex text you out of the blue?

It could be nostalgia, loneliness, curiosity, wanting closure or wanting to get back together. You can't know from one text, which is why it helps to ask or wait.

What do you say when your ex texts happy new year?

A short reply in kind is polite, such as “Thanks, happy New Year to you too.” If you'd rather not engage, it is fine not to reply.

How do you respond when your ex texts merry christmas?

“Merry Christmas, hope you have a relaxing break” is warm and complete. It doesn't invite a longer conversation unless you want one.

How long should you wait to reply to your ex?

As long as you need to know what you want. A few hours or a day is completely normal. There is no rule that you must answer quickly.

How do you tell your ex you don't want to talk?

Kindly and once: say you need no contact to move on and won't be replying. Then follow through. You can mute or block if they keep messaging.

What if my ex texts me and I want to get back together?

Say you'd be open to talking, then have the real conversation in person or on a call. Ask what has changed before picking up where you left off.
