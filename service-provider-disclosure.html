Privacy

The companies that help run Liaison Reply.

Our Privacy Policy explains that we share personal data only as reasonably necessary to host the service, authenticate users, process payments, provide requested outputs, deliver support communications, run analytics, and prevent fraud. This page names the providers we use, what each one does for Liaison Reply, and what information it receives.

Last updated

October 23, 2026.

Scope

This page covers liaisonreply.com and app.liaisonreply.com, the same services as the Privacy Policy.

Key principle

Each provider receives only the information it needs to do its job, and handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Accounts and backend

Supabase

  • What it does: Supabase runs our sign-in, our account database, and the backend functions that power the app.
  • What it receives: Your email address and account details, the conversations, threads, and drafts you save, your settings, and your subscription status.
Payments

Stripe

  • What it does: Stripe processes payments and manages Pro subscriptions, renewals, and cancellations.
  • What it receives: The payment details you enter at checkout, your email address, and subscription and billing information. Card details go directly to Stripe, and Liaison Reply does not store raw payment card data.
App hosting

Vercel

  • What it does: Vercel hosts the Liaison Reply app at app.liaisonreply.com and delivers it to your browser or phone.
  • What it receives: The requests the app makes as you use it, along with standard technical details such as your IP address and browser information.
AI provider

Google (Gemini)

  • What it does: Google's Gemini models are the main AI behind Liaison Reply. They generate your reply options and coaching, and they read the conversation screenshots you upload so the app can rebuild the thread.
  • What it receives: The conversation text you paste or the screenshots you upload, the relationship context and direction you choose, and the draft text you are editing when you use coaching.
AI provider

OpenAI

  • What it does: OpenAI is an additional AI provider. The app can route a generation request to OpenAI when needed, in the same way it uses Google.
  • What it receives: The same conversation text, context, and draft text needed for that request.
AI provider

Anthropic

  • What it does: Anthropic is an additional AI provider. The app can route a generation request to Anthropic when needed, in the same way it uses Google.
  • What it receives: The same conversation text, context, and draft text needed for that request.
Product analytics

PostHog

  • What it does: PostHog records how the app is used, so we can see which features work, which do not, and where people get stuck.
  • What it receives: Usage events from the app, such as features used and actions taken, your plan type, and technical details about your device and browser.
Error reports

Sentry

  • What it does: Sentry collects error reports from the app so we can find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • What it receives: Error details, technical information about your device and browser, and information about what the app was doing when the error happened.
Website analytics

Google Analytics

  • What it does: Google Analytics measures visits to liaisonreply.com, including page views, sessions, and referral patterns.
  • What it receives: Website usage information and device-level details. Analytics storage stays off unless you allow it in the cookie settings.
Website analytics

Microsoft Clarity

  • What it does: Microsoft Clarity helps us understand how visitors use liaisonreply.com, so we can improve the site.
  • What it receives: How you interact with the site, such as pages viewed, clicks, and scrolling, plus device and browser details, subject to the same cookie settings.
Website hosting

GitHub Pages

  • What it does: GitHub Pages hosts liaisonreply.com, including this page.
  • What it receives: Standard web server logs for your visit, such as your IP address and browser details.
Email delivery

Resend

  • What it does: Resend sends service emails on our behalf, such as account and billing related messages.
  • What it receives: Your email address and the content of the emails we send you.
Support email and domain

IONOS

  • What it does: IONOS provides our domain services and hosts the mailboxes for addresses such as support@liaisonreply.com.
  • What it receives: The emails you send to our support addresses, including your email address and the content of your message.
Questions
